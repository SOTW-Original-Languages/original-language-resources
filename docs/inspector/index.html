Corpus build overview

The private research build covers 66 books and uses native BSB and KJV records, followed by KJV-derived NWB and ASV associations. Unresolved associations are excluded from the reader and retained in a separate internal audit.

Research counts: 437,587 BSB source rows; 362,057 KJV native links; 355,067 NWB candidates; 267,439 ASV candidates. These are extracted record counts, not scholarly accuracy rates.
