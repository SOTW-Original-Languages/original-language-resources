Original-language word sequence

Shown in the source’s original-word order, not English word order. Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek forms retain the source’s transliteration and morphology. This preview’s selected passages contain Hebrew and Greek.

“No direct English rendering” is an original-language occurrence, not an error. In Genesis 1:1, English “and” comes from an attached Hebrew waw prefix, not from the H853 object-marker headword.
