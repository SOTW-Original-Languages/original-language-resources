Where this text comes from

ASV: pinned, independently published ASV chapter JSON, with an exact-checked packaged fallback. BSB, KJV and Webster Bible: small passages extracted from original archived source-backed research. Selected BSB original-language rows and native KJV USFM tags are shown separately from carefully selected, exact-word KJV-derived ASV/Webster research links. Derived badges are not the translation’s own original-language tags, and neither type selects a contextual sense.

Webster's English dictionary is loaded from this site's existing Webster 1828 resource, not from a second dictionary copy. Its digital transcription is CC BY-SA 4.0. The BSB is public domain. Detailed scope and source locks: demo README.

Strong's source-authored explanations, Hebrew expanded numbered senses, derivation and KJV renderings are kept distinct. The selected Greek XML contains no separately numbered long-sense list. Dictionary definitions are historical reference material, not a selection of the meaning intended in a specific Bible verse. This demo contains no private research corpus, internal flags, STEP glosses, or CGBC code.
